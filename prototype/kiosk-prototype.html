A clickable mock of the wall-tablet kiosk, built to settle the look and the core loop before any of the real backend exists. Everything here is fake data held in the page — but the interaction is the one the shipped app will have. Tap a face to sign in (any four digits), tick off a chore, then switch to Dad to approve it.

Nobody is signed in — the board still shows everything. That's the point of the kiosk.
